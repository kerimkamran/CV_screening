# SenAI: guide for recruiters (training notes)

SenAI finds and explains what parallax has already analysed. It never hires, rejects or scores anyone; you decide. Ask in Azerbaijani, English or Russian, with or without typos; it answers in your language. Press `/` anywhere to jump to the Ask bar.

## Ten things to try

1. "excel" then "ingilis dili": the second question replaces the skill and shows the resume line where it was found.
2. "həm də SQL": adds a skill to the last search.
3. "only Strong" or "yalnız güclülər": narrows the last list.
4. "compare the first and the second": two or three candidates side by side.
5. "why so few Strong?": which must-have requirement is rarest.
6. "what changed since Monday?": new and changed candidates.
7. "what if Kubernetes were nice to have?": scores again with that change, nothing saved.
8. "interview questions for the first": the same core question per requirement, a rubric and probes from the resume.
9. "but he does know Kubernetes": SenAI searches the resume and tells you what it finds; the stored result stays until you press Re-analyze.
10. "always show top 5": saved; see, change or clear it in the account menu under "What SenAI remembers".

## Reading the answer

Every answer has chips showing what it used (click one to take it off and run again), a table view, Copy, and "How I got this" (how the question was read, rules or AI). A note saying the AI reader was not available means a fixed-rules reading was used; rephrase shortly if the list is not what you meant.

## What it will not do

Judge age, gender, health, family, personality or "will he leave"; look people up on the web or social media; show a salary per candidate; treat a career gap as negative; decide for you. It says so and offers what it can do.

## If it does not know a word

It offers "Suggest this word". An administrator approves it, and from then on SenAI reads it (and its spellings) as a skill.
